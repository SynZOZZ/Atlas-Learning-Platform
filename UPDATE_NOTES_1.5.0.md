# 4Z Academy 1.5.0 — Instructor and Access Control

- Homepage Explore Courses action changed to Login.
- Added an administrator Instructor dashboard with course, material, and student totals.
- Added per-course student records with contact and specialty details.
- Added unique personal access codes bound to one student and one course.
- Administrators select the package, availability in days, allowed section type, and section count.
- Supported packages: full curriculum, midterm review, before midterm, after midterm, and final review.
- Students activate codes in their portal; expired, revoked, or mismatched codes are rejected.
- Course materials can be tagged by package and are filtered by the student's active access.
- Existing Docker data is migrated in place. Do not delete Docker volumes.
